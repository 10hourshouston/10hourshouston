A note to potential partners

Thank you for considering 10 Hours Houston.

This gathering did not begin as an event. It grew out of a sustained culture of prayer, consecration, and spiritual formation cultivated by Every Sphere through Shift 90, Shift 120, the 24-Hour Prayer Retreat, and the 48-Hour Prayer Retreat. Hundreds of hours, held over years, most of them at midnight, with no audience.

Partnering with us is an invitation to help carry that same burden into a public gathering that equips and commissions believers to bear witness to Christ in every space.

Ten hours set apart to seek the Lord, recover ancient wells, and receive strength for new frontiers.

We would be glad to talk through what a partnership could look like for your organisation, including arrangements that are not listed on this page. The partnership form is the quickest way to start the conversation.

Ways To Partner

There is more than one way in.

Partnerships are put together case by case, around what your organisation actually wants out of the day. These are the usual starting points.

Visibility

Your mark on the website, the stage backdrop, printed materials, and across our channels in the run-up to the day.

Presence in the room

Exhibit space at the venue, so your team can meet the people attending face to face across the ten hours.

Hospitality

Underwrite the hall, the food, or refreshments: the practical things that keep a ten-hour gathering running.

Travel support

Help bring speakers in, or help attendees get to Stafford who otherwise could not make the journey.

Tell us which of these fits, or describe something else entirely, and we will put the details together with you.
